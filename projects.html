Project 1 — Median Household Income and its Association with Lava Flow Hazard in Hawaii

May 2026 · ArcGIS Pro, ArcGIS Online, ArcGIS StoryMaps

This project attempts to discover a relationship between lower median household income in Hawaii and susceptibility to lava hazards. It was found that households with lower incomes tend to be more at risk of lava flow hazard, as they are spatially further away from shelters and escape routes.
